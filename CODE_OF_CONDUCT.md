# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to making participation in this project respectful and free from harassment. We welcome people regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, discuss technical disagreements with evidence, give actionable feedback, credit other people’s work, and respect privacy. Do not harass, insult, threaten, or publish another person’s private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Maintainers should investigate fairly, protect confidentiality where possible, and explain corrective actions to affected participants.
